Home is the starting note every drill on this page is measured from. It’s a relative reference, not a page — and not the song’s original key, either. The same home is used for all six songs.

  • home = 0 — the dashed line through the middle of each pitch map.
  • +1, +2… — notes that many semitones above home.
  • −1, −2… — notes that many semitones below home.

Phrases leave home and land back on it — the “steps” line under each map lists every note’s distance from home.

Press “Hear shape” and home sounds at 196 Hz (G3) with the key set to 0. The −1 / −2 / −3 key control transposes the whole shape down, home included — the distances between notes never change.

Train your ear by syllable, not by guesswork.

Movable Do: Do is always home — the tonic of the major scale. Every other syllable is a fixed distance from it, so once you can hear those distances you can find any melody note in any key. Here Do sounds at C4 (261.6 Hz). Tap any syllable to hear it, run the scale, then test yourself.

The major scale — Do to Do

Eight syllables, seven steps. Whole steps everywhere except Mi–Fa and Ti–Do, which are half steps — that squeeze is what makes Fa and Ti so easy to recognize once you listen for it.

Uses the same Piano / Nay / Guitar tones as the song drills.

Anchor on Do

Always start from Do in your head. Don’t chase the note in isolation — walk the scale: Do–Re–Mi… until the pitches match. Speed comes later.

Learn the landmarks

Sol sits strong and open above Do, Fa leans back toward Mi, and Ti pulls hard up to Do. Landmark syllables first, fill in the rest after.

Sing it back

After you answer, sing Do up to the mystery syllable out loud. Hearing it in your own voice locks the distance in faster than tapping alone.

How to read these drills
  • Short, repeatable pitch drills for six karaoke picks.
  • Red line — follow it: higher on the page means higher in your voice.
  • Dashed line = home — your reference pitch. Home counts as 0.
  • +1, +2… — notes that many semitones above home. −1, −2… — below home.
  • Phrases start on home, leave it, and land back on it.
  • Home is the same relative starting note for all six songs — it is not any song’s original key.
  • Press “Hear shape” and home plays at 196 Hz (G3) at key 0. The −1 / −2 / −3 key control moves the whole shape — home included — down by that many semitones.
